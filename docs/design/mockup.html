DONE shows here during sets

Cover = the gym screen

Folded and used one-handed. These are the same four screens as before, stretched to the taller 21:9 cover screen. The extra height goes to a bigger ring and a larger DONE button.

Main = planning + a big live view

The 8″ inner screen is almost square, so every screen splits at the hinge. On the left is what you're doing, and on the right is the context: the circuit plan, the exercise queue and which circuits are done.

Flex mode = phone on the floor

Half-fold the phone and stand it up. The top half shows the timer and the bottom half shows big buttons. That suits push-ups and Nordics, when you can't hold the phone.
